# 20 — Environmental Graphic Language

This is one of the strongest traits extracted from the reference.

Use enormous low-opacity **curved orbital lines**.

They can:

* cross behind the workspace
* frame hero areas
* extend outside viewport boundaries
* intersect structural regions

They represent:

* trajectories
* systems
* orchestration
* context
* connections

Do not make them look like random swooshes.

# 21 — Background Canonical

The core Astra background should be:

**almost-black architectural space**

plus:

* subtle center illumination
* very low green atmospheric tone
* orbital curves
* occasional controlled surface gradient

No photographic scenery is required for the canonical product identity.

This is a major departure from the earlier AIKOV/aurora edition.
