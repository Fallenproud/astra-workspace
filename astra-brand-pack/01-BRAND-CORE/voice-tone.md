# 02 — Canonical Brand Personality

Astra is:

* precise
* calm
* highly capable
* technical
* sophisticated
* architectural
* deliberate
* trustworthy
* extensible
* developer-friendly

Astra is **not**:

* playful consumer AI
* cyberpunk overload
* generic SaaS
* neon gaming UI
* cartoonish
* overly corporate
* decorative sci-fi
* chat-first
