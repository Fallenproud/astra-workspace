# 15 — Typography Hierarchy

### Display

Marketing hero only.

### H1

Page identity.

### H2

Major functional region.

### H3

Card/module heading.

### Body

Human-readable operational copy.

### Label

Small controls and metadata.

### Mono

Machine state, IDs, code, metrics.

Avoid oversized typography inside the core workspace.
